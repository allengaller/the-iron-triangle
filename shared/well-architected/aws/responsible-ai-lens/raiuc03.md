# Refine your use case

Prior to designing or prototyping a solution, imagine the AI
 system solving the use case as just a box containing an unknown
 mechanism. Anticipate the inputs and outputs to the box, how the
 inputs and outputs might vary, and the kind of information (or
 type of AI) the mechanism in the box would need to succeed.
