# Ad measurement and verification

Ad measurement and verification reliability requires robust
 systems to accurately track, validate, and report advertising
 performance metrics while accounting for fraud, viewability, and
 data quality issues. This includes implementing redundant
 verification methods, maintaining data accuracy across multiple
 solutions, and verifying consistent measurement capabilities
 even during system failures or data anomalies.

These reliability practices facilitate consistent and accurate
 ad measurement even during system stress or partial failures,
 while maintaining data quality and measurement integrity across
 the advertising solution.
