# Data management

Data management involves provisioning the minimum amount of
 storage required to meet your workload's needs. For existing
 storage, understand the needs and usage of your data to set
 appropriate lifecycles for your data to move data to the most
 efficient type of storage. Delete unnecessary data, when possible,
 to reduce storage footprint.
