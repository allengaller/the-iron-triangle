# Organization

Life sciences workloads must comply with various regulatory frameworks (FDA, EMA, HIPAA,
 GDPR) depending on application type, data sensitivity, and operational geography. Failure to
 identify applicable frameworks creates operational risk through regulatory incidents and
 potential service disruptions.

Regulatory oversight requires structured governance to maintain regulatory adherence as
 projects evolve and team members change. This includes establishing leadership roles, defining
 oversight processes, and consistently applying regulatory requirements across each project
 phase.

Regulatory requirements extend to vendor solutions, requiring a proactive approach to
 verify regulatory adherence when selecting vendors. When choosing solutions, select only those
 verified as capable of meeting regulatory requirements by the vendor. Avoid trying to make
 non-compliant services fit regulatory requirements.

Life sciences regulations emphasize quality management as a key requirement. Establishing
 IT quality oversight provides the best way to meet IT regulatory requirements
 while maintaining a separation of responsibilities from pharmaceutical quality systems.
