# Performance baselining

In cloud environments, particularly those with shared underlying infrastructure,
 accurately measuring performance baselines for telecommunication workloads can be challenging.
 The lack of visibility into resource utilization at the hardware level and potential
 variations in performance due to multi-tenancy can lead to inaccurate capacity planning. This
 question explores strategies for establishing reliable performance baselines in cloud
 environments to support effective capacity planning for telecommunication workloads.
