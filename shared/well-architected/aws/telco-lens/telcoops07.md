# Geographical redundancy

Telecommunication service providers often face strict requirements for geographical
 redundancy (for example, 200 or more kilometers of separation) while simultaneously needing to
 comply with data residency regulations. This creates unique challenges in cloud environments
 where multiple regions within a single country may not be available. This question explores
 strategies for balancing redundancy needs with regulatory adherence in cloud-based
 telecommunication architectures.
