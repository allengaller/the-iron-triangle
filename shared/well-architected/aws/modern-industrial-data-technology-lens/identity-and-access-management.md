# Identity and access management

Establish secure identity controls and access management patterns between industrial
 systems and human operators in manufacturing environments. This emphasizes proper
 authentication, authorization, and security policies to help protect both IT and OT systems
 while providing appropriate access levels for different roles in the manufacturing
 environment.
