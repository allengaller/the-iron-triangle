# Prepare

Industrial data workloads must comply with relevant regulatory requirements and
 industry standards. Establishing a systematic approach to compliance assessment helps you
 continually adhere to relevant standards while maintaining data integrity and security in
 industrial data infrastructure.

Industrial data infrastructure requires comprehensive monitoring across edge devices,
 networks, and cloud resources for reliable manufacturing operations. Effective observation
 of infrastructure health is crucial for maintaining data quality, system availability, and
 operational continuity from the shop floor to the cloud.
