# AI Workload Documentation

AI workloads use non-deterministic behavior, making them more adaptable to complex situations compared to traditional workloads. They combine code and data into models, creating unique experiences. This guidance focuses on the architectural challenges of designing AI workloads, including non-deterministic functionality, data and application design, and operations.
