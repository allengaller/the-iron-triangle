# Reliability

Standard reliability focuses on recovering from technical failures.
 For sovereign workloads, reliability also covers disruptions from
 regulatory change, trade restrictions, and cross-border recovery
 constraints that can invalidate a recovery strategy entirely.
 Sovereignty controls shape where a workload can fail over, who can
 activate recovery, and what evidence must survive the transition.
 This pillar addresses how you plan for these disruptions, select
 compliant recovery sites, manage third-party sovereignty risks, and
 design workloads for portability across jurisdictions.
