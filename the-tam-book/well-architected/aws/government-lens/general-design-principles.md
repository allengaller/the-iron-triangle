# General design principles

This Government Lens lists high level design principles that
 government customers expect to be applied to services being
 developed with and for them. A version of these principles is found
 in most governments today and in some jurisdictions. Services can be
 audited to verify that they comply with service standards. Each
 principle has one or more questions to consider that support the
 pillar assessment. The principles outlined in this whitepaper draw
 from
 the [Australian
 Digital Service Standard](https://www.dta.gov.au/help-and-advice/about-digital-service-standard), the
 [Canadian
 Digital Standards](https://www.canada.ca/en/government/system/digital-government/government-canada-digital-standards.html), the
 [New
 Zealand Digital Service Standard](https://www.digital.govt.nz/standards-and-guidance/digital-service-design-standard/),
 the [UK
 Government Service Standard](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/service-standard), and the
 [US Government Digital
 Services Playbook](https://playbook.cio.gov/). It’s important that builders are aware of
 the standards for their jurisdiction. It is also worth being aware
 of
 the [Digital
 Nations Charter](https://www.leadingdigitalgovs.org/), first signed in 2014 by the Digital Nations
 cohort, including leading digital government from around the world.

###### The general design principles are:

- [Design with purpose](./design-with-purpose.html)
- [Design with end users (citizens, residents, businesses)](./design-with-end-users-citizens-residents-businesses.html)
- [Make the service simple and intuitive](./make-the-service-simple-and-intuitive.html)
- [Iterate and improve frequently (with end user and staff feedback)](./iterate-and-improve-frequently-with-end-user-and-staff-feedback.html)
- [Collaborate and work in the open by default](./collaborate-and-work-in-the-open-by-default.html)
- [Address security and privacy risks](./address-security-and-privacy-risks.html)
- [Build inclusive services](./build-inclusive-services.html)
- [Design trustworthy services](./design-trustworthy-services.html)
- [Measure, report, and take data-driven decisions](./measure-report-and-take-data-driven-decisions.html)
- [Consider composable architecture and reusability](./consider-composable-architecture-and-reusability.html)
- [Maintain service continuity](./maintain-service-continuity.html)
