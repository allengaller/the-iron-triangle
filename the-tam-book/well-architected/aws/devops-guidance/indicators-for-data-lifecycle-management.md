# Indicators for data lifecycle management

Enforce stringent controls on data
 through its entire lifecycle to ensure residency, privacy,
 sovereignty, and security. Scale data-related processes and
 align them with regulatory compliance, protecting software
 from disruptions due to data mismanagement.
