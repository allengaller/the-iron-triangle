# Security

The security pillar focuses on the ability to protect information,
 systems, and assets through risk assessments and mitigation
 strategies, while also delivering business value to the
 organization. Supply chain operations face unique security
 challenges due to their interconnected nature, involving multiple
 parties, systems, and jurisdictions. Organizations must navigate
 industry-specific requirements and various international trade
 regulations that evolve frequently and vary by region.

Organizations operating global supply chains must comply with
 different security requirements across multiple countries and
 regions, while managing an increasingly complex network of
 suppliers, partners, and service providers.

The security pillar provides an overview of design principles,
 best practices, and questions. You can find implementation
 guidance in the
 [Security
 Pillar whitepaper](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/security-pillar/welcome.html).
