# Incident response

Player misconduct and abusive behaviors can significantly impact the experience of your
 players. Bad player behavior can drive away legitimate players, leading to decreased player
 retention, reduced revenue from in-game purchases, and negative reviews that can damage a
 game's reputation and future sales.

Define policies that promote positive actions among your players and determine how you
 will enforce these policies.
