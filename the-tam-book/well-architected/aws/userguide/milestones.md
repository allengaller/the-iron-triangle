# Milestones

AWS Well-Architected Agent is in preview release and is subject to change.


A milestone records the state of a workload at a particular point in time.

Save a milestone after you initially complete all the questions associated with a workload.
 As you change your workload based on items in your improvement plan, you can save
 additional milestones to measure progress.

A best practice is to save a milestone every time you make improvements to a
 workload.
