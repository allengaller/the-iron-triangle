# Viewing milestones

AWS Well-Architected Agent is in preview release and is subject to change.


You can view milestones for a workload in the following ways:

- On the workload details page, choose **Milestones** and choose the
 milestone you want to view.
- On the **Dashboard** page, choose the workload and in the
 **Milestones** section, choose the milestone you want to
 view.
