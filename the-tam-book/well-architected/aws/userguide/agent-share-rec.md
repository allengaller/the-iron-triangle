# Share recommendations in AWS Well-Architected Agent

AWS Well-Architected Agent is in preview release and is subject to change.


Share specific recommendations with other AWS accounts or team members to collaborate on
 optimization initiatives.

You can share a recommendation by email from either the AWS WA Agent dashboard or the
 recommendation detail page. Both entry points open the same share dialog.

###### To share a recommendation

1. Open the share dialog from one of the following locations:
**From the dashboard:** Choose the radio button next
 to the recommendation you want to share, then choose
 **Share**.**From the recommendation detail page:** Choose
 **Actions** in the top right corner, then **Share
 recommendation**.
2. Enter the email address you want to share the recommendation with.
3. Choose **Share**.
