# Well-Architected Framework Review (WAFR)

AWS Well-Architected Agent is in preview release and is subject to change.


The
 [Well-Architected
 Framework](https://aws.amazon.com/architecture/well-architected/) describes key concepts and design principles for
 creating and running workloads in the cloud. It helps you understand
 the pros and cons of decisions you make while building systems on
 AWS. With the help of the Framework you will learn architectural
 best practices for designing and operating reliable, secure,
 efficient, cost-effective, and sustainable applications.

During the *Well-Architected Framework Review
 (WAFR)*, you will answer a set of foundational questions
 to learn how well your architecture aligns with cloud best practices
 and receive guidance for implementing improvements.

## WAFR phases

The WAFR process consists of three main phases:

- **Prepare:**
 Set up for success with proper planning and stakeholder
 alignment
- **Review:**
 Conduct the actual assessment against AWS best practices
- **Improve:**
 Turn findings into actionable improvements for your workload

This document guides you through the three phases, verifying that
 you have sufficient preparation to derive the most value from the
 review phase. We also guide you towards turning your WAFR findings
 into actionable improvements for your workload.
