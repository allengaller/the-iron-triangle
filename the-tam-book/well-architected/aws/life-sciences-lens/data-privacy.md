# Data privacy

Data privacy requirements vary across the globe. For example, in the US, the handling of
 protected health information (PHI) is governed by [HIPAA](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/laws-regulations/index.html). In the EU, [GDPR](https://gdpr.eu/), [Clinical Trial Regulations](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/human-regulatory/research-development/clinical-trials/clinical-trials-regulation), and others may apply.
